# Privacy Policy: Sparked

Last updated: 18 November 2026

[العربية](./ar)

## 1. Who we are

Sparked is a daily motivation and discipline app owned and operated by Mohammed Badran in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, who is the controller of your personal data.

Privacy contact: support@usesparked.com

## 2. What we collect

Only what the app needs to work:

- Account data: email, name, and how you sign in (email, Apple or Google).
- What you write in the app: daily intentions, tasks, evening reflections, and letters to your future self.
- Usage data: streak count, last check-in date, focus session durations, and your weekly summary.
- Subscription data: your Sparked Pro status and expiry date. We never see your card details. Payment is handled by Apple.
- Notification data: your reminder settings and your device's push token.
- Crash reports: technical details about an error and the device type, used to fix problems.
- Your agreement to this policy: the date you agreed and the policy version.

What we do not collect: your location, contacts or photos. The list of apps you block stays on your device inside Apple's system and never reaches us. If you use the app before signing in, what you write stays on your device and is not sent to us until you create an account.

## 3. How we collect it

Directly from you: when you create an account, when you write in the app, and when you allow notifications. We do not get your data from anyone else, except what Apple or Google send when you sign in through them (email and name).

## 4. Why we use it

- To run your account and save and sync your intentions, tasks and streak.
- To send the reminders you turned on.
- To manage your subscription.
- To fix errors and improve the app.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it for marketing.

## 5. Legal basis

We process your data based on your agreement to this policy when you create your account, and to deliver the service you asked for. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account, and turn off notifications in the app or in your device settings.

## 6. Who we share it with

Service providers that process data on our behalf, for operating the app only:

- Supabase: database and sign-in.
- RevenueCat: subscription management.
- Apple and Google: sign-in, and payments and push delivery through Apple.
- Expo: sending notifications.
- Sentry: crash reports.

We may disclose data when a competent authority requires it under the laws of Saudi Arabia.

## 7. Transfer outside Saudi Arabia

Your data is stored and processed on servers outside Saudi Arabia, including in India and the United States, with the providers listed above. The transfer is needed to provide the service, is limited to the minimum data required, and is protected by contractual and technical safeguards, including encryption in transit and at rest.

## 8. Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your data from the database immediately and permanently, and it leaves our backups within 30 days.

We delete inactive accounts 24 months after last use, and we email you a warning before deletion.

## 9. Your rights

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law you can:

- Know what we collect and why.
- Access your data and get a copy.
- Ask us to correct or complete it.
- Ask us to destroy it.
- Withdraw your consent.

How: account deletion is in the app under Me, then Delete account. For anything else, or if you cannot sign in, email support@usesparked.com and we will reply within 30 days.

## 10. Security

We use encryption in transit, access rules that stop any user from seeing another user's data, and the narrowest possible admin access. If a breach could harm you, we will tell you without delay and notify the competent authority as the law requires.

## 11. Children

Sparked is not directed at anyone under 13. If we learn of an account belonging to a younger child, we will delete it.

## 12. Complaints

If you have a concern, contact us first and we will work to resolve it. You also have the right to complain to the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA).

## 13. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with its date, and tell you in the app when the change is significant.

Policy version: 2.0
